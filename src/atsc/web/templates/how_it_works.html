Semantic match

Every requirement line in the posting is compared with every line of your resume using a small language model that runs on this server. The closest resume line counts for that requirement. A similarity of {{ floor }} maps to 0 and {{ ceiling }} maps to 100; the requirement scores are averaged, with preferred requirements at {{ preferred_weight }} of the weight of required ones. No text is sent to any outside service.

Skill overlap

A curated taxonomy of ML, data science and AI skills groups phrasings that mean the same thing, so "LoRA" and "parameter-efficient fine-tuning" count as one skill. The score is the share of skills the posting names that your resume also names, with each skill weighted by how much the chosen role track values its category, and preferred skills at half weight.

What it does not measure

Anything about you beyond the words on the page. Not culture fit, not seniority fit, not the quality of your work, and not what a recruiter will do next. Two resumes with the same score can have very different outcomes; the score tells you where the words align and where they do not.
